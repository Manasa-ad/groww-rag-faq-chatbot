Mr. Rajinder Singh Loona
B.Sc. and LLB
Mr. Loona is a highly experienced corporate lawyer with expertise in securities market, banking and finance, infrastructure projects, real estate, and regulatory advice. He served as Executive Director (Law) of Securities & Exchange Board of India and oversaw several departments including Legal Affairs, Enforcement, Adjudication, Investigation, and Surveillance. He also worked for IDBI as Chief General Manager (Legal). Mr. Loona has been involved in several expert groups and committees formed by the Government of India and SEBI and founded Alliance Corporate Lawyers, now known as Alliance Law, in 2007.
